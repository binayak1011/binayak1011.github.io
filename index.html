What I do

Three jobs, one habit: find the point where work breaks and fix it there.

The industry changes, the shape of the problem rarely does. Someone has to find the right people, get them productive, and tell the room what is actually happening.

Operations and delivery

Designing the path work takes from brief to sign-off: intake, handoffs, checkpoints, escalation. I am the person who notices which handoff keeps slipping and fixes the process instead of the symptom, then keeps it running while everyone else gets on with their week.
